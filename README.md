summing up Sunday 22 November 2026 in six stories — COP31 climate talks in
overtime in Antalya, the candy factory fire in Banten, the FBI probing a
reported hack of its crime database, Verstappen's wild Las Vegas GP win, a
teen gamer helping the FBI rescue a missing girl, and Theresa May's attack on
Burnham's EU plans — plus four more headlines in the footer.

`draw.py` (Python 3.13, PIL only, no network, deterministic) draws
`entry.png`; `main.ts` copies it to `/output/entry.png`.
